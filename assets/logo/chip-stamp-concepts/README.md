# FactorSeal chip × stamp concepts

Ten monochrome, vector-friendly logo directions. Every mark uses `currentColor`, so it can be recolored from CSS or by setting the SVG `color` property.

1. **Stamp footprint** — the chip body physically becomes a rubber stamp.
2. **Certified chip** — an official seal wrapped around a verified processor.
3. **Press & seal** — the most literal stamp silhouette, with a chip as its base.
4. **Circuit impression** — a round stamp assembled from circuit traces.
5. **Wax chip** — tamper evidence and heritage through a wax-seal shape.
6. **Pin seal** — a compact circular seal with an abstract `F` and radiating pins.
7. **Offset imprint** — the chip and its imperfect physical impression overlap.
8. **Negative stamp** — a stamp handle carved out of the existing chip language.
9. **Shield impression** — certification and hardware security in one badge.
10. **Diamond seal** — a rotated chip that reads as a compact embossed seal.
