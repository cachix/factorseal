03 / EVERY PIXEL COUNTS

Small is its
own design problem.

A dedicated small mark opens up the keyhole and aligns the pins to a 16-pixel grid. Use it at 16–24 px; use the primary mark from 32 px.

Actual display sizes

Keep at least one pin-width of clear space around the visible mark.

Always choose the mark color for the surface beneath it.
