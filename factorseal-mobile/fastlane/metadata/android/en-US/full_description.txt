FactorSeal keeps your passwords and other personal secrets in a vault on your phone. The vault key stays in the phone's secure hardware. You unlock it with your password, your fingerprint or face, or both.

• Logins, notes, cards, identities, SSH keys, API credentials, passports, bank accounts and documents
• Fill and save logins in apps and browsers with Android autofill. Websites only get logins saved for their exact address.
• One-time codes, added from a QR code or a setup link
• A password generator for random passwords and word phrases
• Sync with FactorSeal Desktop and your other devices after pairing them with a QR code. Sync sends only encrypted changes between your devices.
• Import from Bitwarden, 1Password and KeePass, and make encrypted backups that Desktop can restore
• Seals the vault when the screen locks, after an idle timeout and at a maximum unlock time
• Clears copied secrets from the clipboard after 30 seconds

There is no FactorSeal account. Your vault lives only on your devices.

FactorSeal is open source under the Apache 2.0 license. It has not had an independent security audit yet.
